AI Portfolio Builder Prototype

August 2024 - November 2024 Product Design Figma

overview

A collaborative project designing an AI-powered resume and portfolio builder. The project featured extensive user research through interviews and thematic analysis to understand user needs and pain points.

I was responsible for prototyping the home page and user onboarding process, as well as ensuring design cohesion across the entire application. The final deliverable included a high-fidelity prototype, comprehensive reports, and a video presentation detailing the design process and future improvements.

my role

Led the design of the home page and user onboarding flow, conducted user interviews and thematic analysis, created paper and digital prototypes, and ensured visual and interaction consistency across all screens.

tools & skills

Figma, Paper Prototyping, Digital Prototyping, User Research, User Testing, Critical Analysis, Design Systems
